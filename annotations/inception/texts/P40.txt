feat: do not delete frame children on frame delete

This PR changes how frame deletion works. Previously, we deleted the frame and its children. Now we're only deleting the frame and keeping the children, selecting them instead.

This way it's much easier to remove children from the frame instead of having to do it through context menu in two steps.

If people want to delete both the frame and its children both, they can just hit delete twice.

(Un)related, fixed (or rather, improved, as a proper fix will take more work) selection on removing the last group member when editing a group.
